# READ 2:14 — Story Bible

> **Spoilers.** This document contains the full solution.

## Logline
A disgraced true-crime podcaster gets a message from an anonymous number: a 19-year-old girl in a fog-bound harbour town has been missing nine days, the police say she ran away, and the sender has cloned her phone. The goodbye text she "sent" at 2:14 am wasn't written by her — and she may still be alive.

## Pitch (3 lines)
Ivy Calder vanished from Saltmere after a goodbye text at 2:14 am. Police closed the case as a runaway.
An anonymous contact called TIDE puts her cloned phone on your home screen and asks the one person who knows what being wrong costs — you.
Read her messages, break her codes, interrogate a town full of liars, and find out what she discovered about the ferry that killed her mother… before the tide turns.

## Setting
**Saltmere** — a small, grey fishing town on a northern coast. Fog most nights. A brick cannery on a pier, a ferry terminal with old coin lockers, a harbour office with a basement full of logbooks. Offshore: **Gull Rock**, a tidal island with a Victorian **fog-signal station**, locked since 2011, reachable on foot only across a causeway at low tide.

Six years ago (17 March 2020) the ferry **MV Merrow (SM-4127)** sank two miles off Gull Rock. Four died, including **Nora Calder**, Ivy's mother. The inquiry blamed a "freak wave".

Present day: **Monday 12 October 2026**, night. Ivy has been missing since the early hours of **Saturday 3 October**.

## The player — Rowan Hale
Former host of the true-crime podcast **Dead Air**. In episode 39 Rowan named an innocent man, Peter Lusk, as a suspect; the real culprit was found later. Episode 40 was an on-air apology; the show ended. Rowan now edits wedding videos. Gender is never specified. Ivy was a fan and emailed Rowan after episode 40. Rowan never replied.

**Motive:** Exposure of the falsified inspection would mean four manslaughter charges and the end of Danner's life as Saltmere's beloved harbour master.

**How Danner learns about TIDE:** Rowan mentions an anonymous source to Brennan; Brennan, Danner's Sunday golf partner, mentions it to him.

## The solution (what the player must deduce)
1. Ivy didn't write the 02:14 message (capitals + signature; she never does either).
2. A conversation was deleted at 02:11 → whoever took the phone was in it.
3. The car was moved after the message by someone tall (seat back, CCTV).
4. Ivy found the falsified Merrow inspection signed W.D.
5. The dark skiff was HM-2 — only Danner takes it out; he lied ("young Hal", who's in a cast).
6. Danner knew the message was signed — something only the police, family and the writer could know.
7. Ivy's band synced yesterday via relay R7 = Gull Rock fog station; causeway opens 03:40.

## Chapters

### Chapter 1 — "Read 2:14"
TIDE contacts Rowan, pushes them with the Peter Lusk guilt, shares the Herald article, installs Ivy's cloud mirror. Maya (trust branch: honest vs. pretend to be police) reveals Ivy's passcode habit. TIDE sends the Merrow plaque photo → **Puzzle 1**. Ivy's phone opens (family chat, Maya, Jonah, "goblin 🦀", notes, photos). **Puzzle 2** proves the 2:14 message is fake; the deleted-thread notice and "pls come now" escalate it to abduction. Walt Danner introduces himself, warm and helpful. TIDE: "careful who you talk to."

### Chapter 2 — "Low Tide"
Jonah (hard vs. easy approach; going hard gets you blocked until TIDE intervenes) gives his alibi timecard and the dark-skiff sighting. **Puzzle 3** exposes Maya's lie; she confesses the kiss and Ivy's "meeting". New notes reveal the falsified inspection and "the handwriting on my birthday cards". Walt claims he was asleep by eleven and asks whether Ivy "left any papers". Brennan dismisses you but sends the CCTV still. **Puzzle 4** rebuilds the timeline. Ivy's last note → **Puzzle 5** (locker 4127). **Key branch:** send TIDE to the terminal *now* (logbook secured) or *wait for daylight* (locker pried open, logbook lost). Walt warns you off "people who hide behind made-up names".

### Chapter 3 — "Fog Signal"
A harbour truck idles outside TIDE's house. Ivy's Pulse app shows a sync *yesterday* — she's alive. Jonah identifies HM-2. Walt slips: "signing off with her name like that". (Branch: call him out → he knows you know.) **Puzzle 6**: relay R7 = Gull Rock; causeway opens 03:40. **Puzzle 7 (optional, one guess):** who is TIDE? If Theo, he confesses; you can urge him to tell his dad. **Final:** accuse (Walt / Brennan / Jonah / Maya) and choose who to trust (County CID + coastguard / Brennan / go alone).

## Endings
- **Bad — "Still Water":** wrong accusation, trusting Brennan (he phones Danner), or sending TIDE alone. Ivy is never found.
- **Good — "Low Tide":** Danner accused + County CID/coastguard. Ivy rescued at 03:47; Danner charged with kidnapping. Text varies if the logbook was lost (Merrow stays a "freak wave"), TIDE wasn't identified, or Theo stayed silent.
- **True — "The Fog Lifts":** Good + logbook secured (go *now* in Ch2) + TIDE correctly identified + Theo tells his dad. Martin leads the rescue across the causeway; Merrow inquiry reopened; Ivy texts Rowan: "you answered." Dead Air returns with two hosts.

## Red herrings
- **Jonah:** "ur gonna regret this" (clarified 4 min later), hosted the bonfire, short fuse → airtight timecard alibi; he's the witness who saw HM-2.
- **Maya:** lied about being home → hiding a kiss, not a crime.
- **Brennan:** closed the case fast, sat on the Merrow inquiry, leaks to Danner → lazy and too cosy, not the kidnapper.
- **TIDE:** evasive, knows too much → a guilty 16-year-old brother.

## Themes
Being wrong in public vs. lying in private. Who gets believed in a small town. "The sea keeps what the town remembers."
